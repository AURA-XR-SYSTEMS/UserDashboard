Good to know

New members: your first month is free. You won't be charged for 30 days, and you can cancel anytime before then. Membership starts in Stripe Checkout, which asks for a card; your free month converts to a paid monthly membership automatically when it ends. Cancel anytime from Billing. Your account updates after Stripe confirms the subscription.

Buying for a team? The team plan puts several seats on one bill, and you invite each person by email.

Billing questions or need help? Email support@auraengine.com. If you believe you were charged in error, contact us before filing a dispute with your bank: we can review the charge, cancel future billing, and check refund eligibility. Refund and cancellation terms are in our Terms of Service.
